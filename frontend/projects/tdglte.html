CREATIVE WEB PLATFORM · 03

MysticPenHD
/ TDGLTE

A digital publishing experience built around an original novel universe, bringing storytelling, chapters, characters, world-building, and community features into one platform.

RoleCreator · Developer
FocusDigital publishing
StackWeb · Node.js · MongoDB
StatusActive project

THE IDEA

Give an original story a digital home that can grow with it.

MysticPenHD / TDGLTE combines creative world-building with a custom web experience. The goal is to make the story itself the center of the product while giving readers useful ways to explore its chapters, characters, setting, and wider universe.

The platform also explores publishing tools and community interactions, turning a static story website into an evolving creative system.

KEY AREAS

01

Story archive

Readers can move through the novel and its chapter-based publishing experience.

02

World-building

Characters and setting information extend the reading experience beyond individual chapters.

03

Publishing tools

Content management capabilities support chapters, characters, rich text, and creative updates.

04

Community

Reader interaction features help turn the story platform into a place for ongoing engagement.
