THE EXPERIENCE

Build a complete fan-community product, not just a landing page.

This project explores how a fan community can combine content, membership, events, commerce, and account features in one cohesive experience.

The application includes multiple membership tiers, meeting and gift experiences, payment flows, role-aware accounts, and a responsive digital membership card designed as part of the product rather than an afterthought.

KEY AREAS

01

Membership

Tiered membership experiences with member information and digital card presentation.

02

Payments

Payment integration and callback verification were developed as part of the membership purchase flow.

03

Community

Accounts, events, meetings, gifts, and community-facing pages bring the experience together.

04

Product design

Responsive layouts and visual hierarchy were treated as product requirements alongside the technical implementation.
