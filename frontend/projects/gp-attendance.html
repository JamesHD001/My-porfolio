Attendance

An attendance management platform designed to make class attendance easier to organize, record, review, and administer across different user roles.

RoleDeveloper
FocusAttendance workflows
StackJavaScript · Firebase
StatusActive project

THE PROBLEM

Make attendance practical for different responsibilities.

The platform is designed around the reality that administrators, instructors, moderators, and leaders need different levels of access. Instead of treating attendance as a single workflow, the system separates responsibilities through role-based dashboards and class-specific actions.

The project has evolved through hands-on iteration, including instructor attendance, general attendance, class management, student assignment, and moderator workflows.

KEY AREAS

01

Role-based access

Different dashboards and permissions for administrators, instructors, moderators, and leaders.

02

Class attendance

Tools for marking attendance for assigned individual classes and shared general classes.

03

Administration

Class and student management workflows for the users responsible for maintaining the system.

04

Operational flexibility

Moderator workflows help keep attendance moving when an instructor forgets or cannot complete a marking task.
