THE CONCEPT

Make reading more social without losing the book itself.

Hi!Book is a product concept for a social reading and publishing environment where readers and creators can have profiles, discover books, participate in communities, and interact around the stories they care about.

The project is also an exploration of product architecture and user experience, with ideas for identity, country-aware onboarding, digital currency, publishing, and community participation.

KEY AREAS

01

Reader profiles

Profiles provide a foundation for identity and participation within the reading community.

02

Books & publishing

The concept connects readers and creators through books and digital publishing workflows.

03

Community

Social interaction is treated as part of the reading experience rather than a separate destination.

04

Digital ecosystem

Ideas such as digital currency and platform services create room for a broader product ecosystem.
