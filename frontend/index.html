ABOUT ME

I work across engineering and creative disciplines.

I like taking an idea from a rough concept to something people can actually experience.

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, web development, graphic design, writing, and composition. I approach each discipline as another way to communicate: code creates the system, design shapes the experience, and storytelling gives an idea its voice.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering while building real projects that challenge me to think about architecture, usability, visual hierarchy, content, and the people who will use what I create.

I value practical problem-solving, continuous learning, clean interfaces, and work that has a clear purpose. My portfolio is intentionally a mix of technical and creative projects because both sides are part of how I build.
